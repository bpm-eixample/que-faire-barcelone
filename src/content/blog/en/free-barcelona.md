Barcelona can be enjoyed without opening your wallet. Here are our best ideas for free activities.

## Beaches and the seafront

The **Barceloneta**, Poblenou and the long seaside promenade: swimming, strolling and sunsets, all free.

## Parks and viewpoints

**Park Güell** (free outer area), **Montjuïc** hill and the **Carmel bunkers** offer spectacular panoramas at no cost.

## Free museums (at certain times)

Many museums are free on the **first Sunday of the month** or some afternoons (MNAC, Picasso Museum, MUHBA…). Check the slots.

## Wander the neighbourhoods

Getting lost in the **Barri Gòtic**, El Born or Gràcia, admiring the modernist façades of **Passeig de Gràcia**: Barcelona's finest activity is free.

## Markets and atmosphere

The **Boqueria** and local markets: a festival of colours and smells, with no need to buy.
