Three days is the ideal format to taste the essentials of Barcelona without rushing. Here's our tried-and-tested itinerary.

## Day 1 — The historic heart

Start with the **Gothic Quarter** and the cathedral, wander down the **Ramblas** and stop at the **Boqueria** market. In the afternoon, head to **El Born** and the Picasso Museum.

## Day 2 — Modernism

Spend the morning at the **Sagrada Família** (book early!), then head up to **Park Güell**. In the evening, a tapas tour in **Gràcia**.

## Day 3 — Sea and views

Relax at **Barceloneta**, enjoy a seafood lunch, then spend the afternoon on **Montjuïc** hill for the gardens and the view.

> Tip: buy skip-the-line tickets in advance for the major sites — you'll save hours.
