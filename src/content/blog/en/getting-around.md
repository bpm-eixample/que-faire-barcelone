Barcelona is a compact, well-connected city. Here's how to get around easily and at the best price, from the airport to the alleys of the old town.

## From the airport

**El Prat** airport is about 15 km from the centre. Three main options:

- **Aerobus** — the direct shuttle to Plaça de Catalunya in ~35 min, with very frequent departures. Around **€7 one-way** (indicative price). **It's the best option if you travel alone or as a pair.**
- **Taxi** — door to door, faster and no juggling your luggage. Around **€30–40** depending on traffic and terminal. **From 3 people, it's the best value for money and time.**
- **Metro (L9 Sud)** — the cheapest option, but with connections and more time. A specific airport ticket is required.

> Tip: with 3–4 people and suitcases, a taxi often costs barely more than the Aerobus per person, while being far more comfortable.

## Getting around the city

- **Taxi** — plentiful, reliable and **cheap compared to other major** European cities. Handy in the evening or with luggage.
- **Metro** — fast and efficient. **Go for the 10-trip ticket (T-Casual)** rather than the 24h / 48h tourist passes (Hola Barcelona) unless you plan a lot of trips: it's usually much cheaper.
- **Shared bikes** — available through apps like **Bolt**, **Uber** and others. Ideal for the seafront and flat routes.
- **Electric scooters** — self-service with **Yego**, **Acciona**, Cooltra, etc. Great for zipping between neighbourhoods (licence may be required depending on the model).

## …and above all, on foot

For **short distances, walking is best**. Each neighbourhood has its own architecture and atmosphere — from the Gothic maze to the modernist avenues of the Eixample — and with Barcelona's **great weather**, strolling is often the most enjoyable option… and it's free.
