Barcelona is an ideal family destination: sea, parks and fun activities within easy reach. Here are our must-dos with kids.

## The beaches

The **Barceloneta** and the less crowded Poblenou beaches are perfect for a swim and sandcastles.

## Parks to let off steam

The **Parc de la Ciutadella** (rowing boats on the lake) and **Parc de les Glòries** (playgrounds, lawns for picnics) will delight the little ones.

## The Montjuïc cable car

Riding the cabin above the city is always a hit, with a superb view at the top.

## The Aquarium and the port

The **Barcelona Aquarium** at Port Vell and the seafront stroll appeal to all ages.

## Tibidabo

The city's oldest amusement park, perched on the hill, with vintage rides and a panoramic view.

> Tip: alternate cultural visits with outdoor downtime to keep kids' energy up. See also our **Free & outdoors** section.
