Barcelona is a great base for exploring Catalonia. Here are our favourite day trips, all doable in a day.

## Montserrat — the sacred mountain

About an hour from the city, this dramatic massif is home to a Benedictine monastery and breathtaking views. Reach it by train + cable car, or on a guided tour.

## Sitges — the chic seaside town

Just 40 minutes by train takes you to this pretty coastal town, with its beaches, whitewashed centre and relaxed vibe.

## Costa Brava — coves and turquoise water

To the north, the Costa Brava is dotted with wild coves and fishing villages like Calella de Palafrugell or Tossa de Mar. Ideal in summer.

## Girona — the medieval city

Cobbled lanes, city walls, the Jewish quarter and colourful houses along the Onyar. One hour by high-speed train.
