For three February nights, the industrial streets of **Poblenou** turn into an open-air art gallery. **Llum BCN**, Barcelona's light art festival, fills them with around thirty works of light: projections on façades, interactive structures, plays of reflection in the parks. It's **free**, no booking needed, and one of the best things to do in Barcelona in winter.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dates** | Planned for Friday 5 to Sunday 7 February 2027 (16th edition) |
| **Opening times** | Friday and Saturday 7 pm – midnight, Sunday 7 pm – 11 pm (2026 times) |
| **Where** | Poblenou and around Glòries |
| **Price** | Free, no booking |
| **Time needed** | 2–3 hours for a full route |

## What is Llum BCN?

Born as the light festival of the Santa Eulàlia celebrations, Llum BCN moved to **Poblenou**, the former industrial district turned hub of innovation and design. Every year, **local and international artists** share the streets with **students from the city's design, art and architecture schools**: in 2026, 12 artists and 18 schools created more than thirty installations. Almost all of them are outdoors.

The fun is in the mix: a monumental piece projected onto an old factory, then, round the corner, a student installation you can touch, walk through or set off with your voice.

## The routes

In 2026 the festival was laid out as **three colour-coded routes**:
- **The blue route**, around the **Disseny Hub** (the Design Museum), at the foot of the Glòries tower;
- **The orange route**, around **Plaça de les Glòries** and its surroundings;
- **The green route**, towards **Parc del Poblenou**, by the sea.

The 2027 map is published by the city a few days before the festival on barcelona.cat/llumbcn — we'll share it on our [Events](/en/evenements/) page. Tip: pick **one or two routes** rather than trying to see everything in one night.

## Practical tips

- **Go on Friday or Sunday.** On Saturday night some installations have 20–30-minute queues.
- **Arrive at 7 pm**, as night falls: it's the quietest time and the light is perfect for photos.
- **By metro**: L1 to **Glòries**, or L4 to **Poblenou** or **Llacuna**.
- **Comfortable shoes** and a warm jacket: you'll walk a lot, and evenings are 8–10 °C.
- **With kids** it's ideal: many installations are interactive. Go early in the evening.
- **Indoor installations** are few but popular: do them first.
