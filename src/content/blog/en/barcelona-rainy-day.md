Raining in Barcelona? No worries: the city is full of indoor activities. Here are our ideas.

## Museums

The Picasso Museum, Fundació Joan Miró, MNAC or the History Museum of Barcelona: plenty to keep you dry and warm for hours.

## Covered markets

The **Boqueria** or the Sant Antoni market: wander between the stalls, snack and stay sheltered at the same time.

## Shopping

**Passeig de Gràcia** for the shops, or **Las Arenas** (a mall inside a former bullring) with its covered panoramic terrace.

## Gaudí indoors

A visit to the **Sagrada Família** or **Casa Batlló** is just as enjoyable in the rain — remember to book a skip-the-line ticket.

## Take shelter over a good meal

A long tapas lunch, a cooking workshop or a cocktail class: rain is the perfect excuse for a tasty break.

> Good to know: showers in Barcelona are often short. Keep a small umbrella handy and adapt your plans as the day goes on.
