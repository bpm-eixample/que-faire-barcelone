A **bachelor or bachelorette party** in Barcelona largely comes down to Saturday night. The difficulty isn't finding a good bar — the city has hundreds — but finding one that will **take twelve people at 11pm on a Saturday**. That's a completely different problem, and it's the one this guide solves.

## How a night in Barcelona actually runs

The local rhythm catches out visiting groups, who start too early and run out of steam before the good part.

- **7–9pm: vermouth.** On a terrace, with olives and tinned seafood. The easiest moment of the night to do as a large group.
- **9–11pm: dinner.** Nobody eats before nine here. Book, always.
- **11pm–1:30am: cocktails.** The heart of the night, and where Barcelona genuinely excels.
- **1:30–5am: the club.** Before 1am they're empty. Turning up at midnight marks you as unbriefed.

A group that starts at 7:30pm and hits the club at 1:30am lasts comfortably. One that starts at 5pm does not.

## The landmark cocktail bars

Barcelona is one of Europe's great cocktail cities. The bars below are institutions — excellent, but small, so best visited in a smaller group or early.

- **Paradiso** (El Born) — a speakeasy behind a pastrami counter, regularly ranked among the world's best. Expect a queue at weekends.
- **Sips** (Eixample) — the city's other big name, with a highly technical approach.
- **Dr. Stravinsky** (El Born) — house distillates and infusions, apothecary decor, warm atmosphere.
- **Two Schmucks** (Raval) — looser, younger, superb value for the atmosphere.
- **Boadas** (Barri Gòtic) — opened in 1933, the city's oldest. Standing room, old school, no concessions.

⚠️ In practice, none of these will take a booking for fifteen people on a Saturday. For a group they work as a **second stop, in smaller sub-groups** — not as a meeting point.

## The venues that genuinely welcome groups

This is where most stag and hen weekends come unstuck. What to look for: room to sit, a simple menu, and staff used to large tables.

- **BPM Bar (Eixample, Aragó 127)** — the gathering point for Barcelona's French-speaking community, and one of the few places where twelve of you can arrive without overwhelming the bar. Multilingual team, house cocktails, and **cocktail workshops** that let you start the night on the spot before moving on. Our default recommendation as a group meeting point.
- **BPM Bar — Encants** — the second address, near Glòries, quieter, useful if you're staying east of the centre.
- **The vermouth bars of Gràcia and Sant Antoni** — big terraces, gentle prices, perfect for a large-group aperitivo before heading into the centre.
- **Hotel rooftops** — La Isabela, Terraza Martínez and similar will take groups with a booking, with the view thrown in. Pricier, but unbeatable at sunset.

All our addresses, with Google Maps links, are on the [Nightlife](/en/vie-nocturne/) page.

## The clubs

- **Razzmatazz** (Poblenou) — five rooms, five moods, from indie to techno. The best choice for a group with mixed taste: everyone finds their room.
- **Sala Apolo** (Paral·lel) — sharp programming in a former ballroom, with real character.
- **Opium and Pacha** (seafront) — big-format, very touristy, very effective in summer.
- **La Terrrazza** (Poble Espanyol) — open air, summer only, and one of the best nights available in the city.

**What you need to know about the door.** Large all-male groups get turned away regularly, without explanation. Three ways around it: book a table or guest list in advance, arrive in sub-groups of three or four a few minutes apart, or mix the group. ID is checked every time, and it has to be the original document.

## What will get you refused

- **Stag and hen costumes and props**: after several city campaigns against booze tourism, many venues now turn costumed groups away.
- A group that **arrives visibly drunk**: door selection is real.
- Shorts and flip-flops at the seafront clubs.
- Showing up **fifteen at once** with no warning.

## The running order we recommend

1. **7:30pm** — vermouth on a terrace in Gràcia or Sant Antoni.
2. **9pm** — booked tapas dinner (see our [where to eat](/en/ou-manger/) picks).
3. **11pm** — meeting point at a bar that can take the whole group.
4. **12:30am** — split into sub-groups towards the landmark cocktail bars.
5. **1:30am** — club, with a table or guest list booked.
