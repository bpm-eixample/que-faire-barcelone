In Barcelona, the aperitif is a way of life. Here's how to enjoy it like a local.

## Vermouth, the institution

"Fer el vermut" (having the aperitif) is a Catalan tradition, especially on weekend late mornings. Served with an olive and a slice of orange, alongside a few tapas.

## Cava, the Catalan bubbles

**Cava**, the sparkling wine made in Catalonia, is the local alternative to champagne — perfect for a toast on a terrace at a gentle price.

## Tapas, to share

Patatas bravas, pa amb tomàquet, croquettes, anchovies, ham… You order several **raciones** to share rather than a plate each. The best way to taste a bit of everything.

## Where to go

The neighbourhoods of **Gràcia**, **Sant Antoni** and **El Born** are full of authentic tapas bars. For a guided experience (cava, cocktails and tapas in a real local bar), check our **activities to book**.

> Tip: shift your aperitif to 7–8 pm, Spanish time — bars get lively much later than elsewhere.
