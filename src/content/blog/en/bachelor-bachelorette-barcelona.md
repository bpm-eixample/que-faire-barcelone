Barcelona is **Europe's #1 destination** for a **bachelor** or **bachelorette party** (stag / hen party): sun, sea, food, legendary nightlife and cheap flights. Here's how to plan an unforgettable weekend.

## Where to go out: bars & clubs

For the nightlife, head to:
- **Signature cocktail bars**: Paradiso, Sips, Two Schmucks, Dr. Stravinsky.
- **Nightclubs**: Razzmatazz, Opium, Sala Apolo, La Terrrazza (summer).
- **Rooftops at sunset**: La Isabela, Terraza Martínez.

All in our [Nightlife section](/en/vie-nocturne/), with direct Google Maps links.

## Original group activities

- **Cocktail & sangria workshop** — get hands-on in a real Barcelona bar.
- **Sunset catamaran cruise** (drinks + music).
- **Bike tour or giant pedaller** to explore the city with a laugh.
- **Treasure hunt / escape game** in the Gothic Quarter.
- **Flamenco class** or paella cooking class together.

👉 See all our **[activities to book](/en/activites/#reserver)** — many are perfect for groups.

## Practical tips

- **Book early**: good hotels and restaurants fill up fast on weekends.
- **Stay together**: an Airbnb or hotel with several rooms, in **Eixample** or **El Born** for a quiet central base.
- **Getting in from the airport**: the **Aerobús** is ideal for 1–2 people; taxi/rideshare from 3+ people (see our [transport guide](/en/blog/getting-around/)).
- **ID required**: bars and clubs often check IDs at the door.

## Skip the tourist traps

Avoid over-touristy "stag party packages": Barcelona's best nights are in the real local bars — more authentic, cheaper and much more fun.

> **Ready to book?** Combine a creative workshop in the afternoon + a tapas dinner + a bar & club crawl in the evening: the winning recipe for a bachelor or bachelorette party in Barcelona.
