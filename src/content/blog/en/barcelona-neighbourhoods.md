Barcelona is best experienced neighbourhood by neighbourhood, each with its own personality. Here's a quick guide to where to stay, wander and go out.

## Barri Gòtic — the historic heart

Medieval lanes, hidden squares and the cathedral: the Gothic Quarter is the oldest and most touristy. Beautiful early in the morning, before the crowds.

## El Born — bohemian and trendy

Right next to the Gòtic, El Born blends artisan workshops, designer shops, wine bars and the Picasso Museum. Perfect for an evening out.

## Eixample — Gaudí and shopping

The grid district of Modernism: Sagrada Família, Casa Batlló, Casa Milà and Passeig de Gràcia for shopping. Central and well connected.

## Gràcia — the village vibe

Quieter and more open, Gràcia is a former village with friendly squares and tapas bars popular with locals.

## La Barceloneta & the seafront

The old fishermen's quarter, the beach and seafood restaurants. A seaside feel just steps from the centre.

## Poblenou — the local Barcelona

A former industrial area turned creative hub: street art, a quiet rambla and less crowded beaches. Perfect for seeing the city like a local.

> Where to stay? The **Eixample**, **Sant Antoni** or around the Sagrada Família are central yet calmer than the Gòtic.
