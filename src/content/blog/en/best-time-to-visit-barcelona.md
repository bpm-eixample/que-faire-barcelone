Barcelona can be visited year-round thanks to its mild Mediterranean climate. But depending on your priorities — weather, crowds or budget — some periods are better than others.

## Spring (April–June) — the best time

Mild temperatures, sunny days and blossoming greenery. **May and June** are ideal: great weather, a warming sea and major festivals. Our favourite time.

## Summer (July–August) — beach and buzz

Hot and festive, perfect for the beach… but also high season: crowds, sometimes heavy heat and higher prices. Book early.

## Autumn (September–October) — the sweet spot

The sea is still warm, temperatures pleasant and crowds thin out. **September** is excellent, with the big **La Mercè** festival.

## Winter (November–March) — quiet and cheap

Mild compared to the rest of Europe, few crowds and low prices. Great for cultural visits, though swimming will have to wait.

> Tip: whatever the season, book popular sights (Sagrada Família, Park Güell) ahead. See our **activities to book** section.
