First trip to Barcelona? Here are the essential tips to avoid the classic pitfalls and make the most of it.

## Book the big sights in advance

The **Sagrada Família** and **Park Güell** often sell out. Book your skip-the-line tickets several days, even weeks, ahead.

## Watch out for pickpockets

Barcelona is safe, but petty theft happens, especially on **Las Ramblas**, in the metro and crowded spots. Keep your bag closed in front of you and your phone away.

## Get the right transport ticket

Choose the **10-trip ticket (T-Casual)** over 24/48h passes unless you make lots of trips. (See our transport article.)

## Adopt Spanish timings

Lunch is around 2 pm and dinner rarely before 9 pm. Bars get lively late: shift your rhythm.

## Don't stay only on Las Ramblas

The real highlights are in the neighbourhoods: Gràcia, El Born, Poblenou… (See our neighbourhood guide.)

## Walk

The centre is compact and beautiful on foot. Save the metro for longer distances.

> In short: book tickets ahead, watch your belongings, and let the neighbourhoods guide you.
