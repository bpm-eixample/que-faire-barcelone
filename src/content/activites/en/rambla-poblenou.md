## Why go

Far quieter than the famous Ramblas downtown, the **Rambla del Poblenou** gently leads down to the sea. Tree-lined and dotted with terraces and small shops, it has a genuine **local neighbourhood** feel loved by Barcelonians.

## Our tips

- Perfect for a relaxed walk down to Poblenou beach.
- Stop at one of the many terraces or ice-cream parlours.
- The Poblenou district is also known for its street art and converted old factories.

## Getting there

Metro L4 **Poblenou** stop.
