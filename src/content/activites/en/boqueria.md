## Why go

The **Mercat de la Boqueria** is an explosion of colours and flavours: fresh fruit, jamón, seafood, pressed juices... A must for foodies, right next to the Ramblas.

## Our tips

- Go in the morning, when the stalls are at their fullest.
- Try a fresh juice and snack at the market's tapas counters.
- Avoid Sundays: the market is closed.

## Getting there

Metro L3 **Liceu** stop, on the Rambla.
