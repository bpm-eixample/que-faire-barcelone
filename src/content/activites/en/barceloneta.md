## Why go

Just steps from the centre, **Barceloneta** is the city's most iconic beach. Swimming, a stroll along the promenade, seafood restaurants and beach bars — the Mediterranean atmosphere at its best.

## Our tips

- Watch out for street vendors and keep an eye on your belongings.
- For authentic paella, head a little away from the seafront.
- At sunset, go up to the **W Hotel** for a great view.

## Getting there

Metro L4 **Barceloneta** stop, then a 10 min walk to the sea.
