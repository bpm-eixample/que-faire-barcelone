## Why go

**Parc de les Glòries** is a vast, recently landscaped green space around the square of the same name. Lawns, shaded areas and playgrounds make it the perfect spot to **picnic**, read or simply relax in the sun between sights.

## Our tips

- Bring some snacks: it's one of the best spots for a quiet picnic.
- Steps from the **Glòries** tower and the Design Museum (DHUB).
- Plenty of space for families and kids.

## Getting there

Metro L1 **Glòries** stop.
