## Why go

**Las Arenas** is a shopping centre set inside an early-20th-century former **bullring**, whose circular brick façade has been preserved. Shops, restaurants and a cinema sit under a dome, and the **rooftop terrace** offers a great view over Plaça d'Espanya and Montjuïc.

## Our tips

- Head up to the circular rooftop (free access from outside, or paid lift) for the view.
- Ideal if it's raining or very hot.
- Combine it with Montjuïc's **Magic Fountain**, right next door.

## Getting there

Metro L1/L3 **Espanya** stop.
