## Why go

**Passeig de Gràcia** is Barcelona's most elegant avenue: international brands, luxury boutiques and designers line the street. It's also a showcase of **modernist architecture**, with Gaudí's Casa Batlló and Casa Milà (La Pedrera).

## Our tips

- Perfect for a shopping session, from high street to luxury brands.
- Look up: the modernist façades are as worthwhile as the shop windows.
- Plenty of cafés for a break between stores.

## Getting there

Metro L2/L3/L4 **Passeig de Gràcia** stop.
