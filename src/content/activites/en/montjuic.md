## Why go

Overlooking the harbour, **Montjuïc** offers lush gardens, the historic castle, the MNAC and the **Magic Fountain** show. The cable car offers a spectacular view over the city.

## Our tips

- Take the cable car (*Telefèric de Montjuïc*) up and walk back down through the gardens.
- Check the **Magic Fountain** schedule (weekend evenings, seasonal).
- Bring water: the hill is large and sunny.

## Getting there

Metro L2/L3 **Paral·lel** stop, then the Montjuïc funicular.
