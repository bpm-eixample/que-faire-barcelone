In Catalonia, 31 October is mostly about **roasted chestnuts**, **sweet potatoes** and **panellets** (little almond-and-pine-nut sweets) washed down with moscatel wine. The *castanyeres* set up their braziers on the street, bakeries fill up with panellets, and **1 November (Tots Sants) is a public holiday** — many shops close, so plan your shopping ahead.
