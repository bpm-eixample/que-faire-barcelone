Held around Saint Michael's Day (29 September), the neighbourhood's patron saint, Barceloneta's festival still feels like a village party: **havaneres** sea shanties with burnt rum, sardana dancing, the giants' parade, pétanque tournaments and a **correfoc** on the second weekend. This year a new giant pays tribute to flamenco dancer **Carmen Amaya**, born in the neighbourhood.
