For Saint Anthony, patron saint of animals, riders and horse-drawn carriages loop three times around the neighbourhood (*els tres tombs*) to receive the blessing. Brass bands, period costume and a village atmosphere right in the middle of the city. **Sant Antoni** also happens to be one of the best neighbourhoods in Barcelona for a Sunday vermouth.
