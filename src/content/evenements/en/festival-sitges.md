Since 1968 **Sitges** has hosted one of the world's most respected genre film festivals: premieres, horror films, midnight screenings and a whole town that plays along, zombies included. The 59th edition runs for eleven days. R2 Sud train from Passeig de Gràcia or Sants, about 40 minutes.
