Barcelona's architecture festival opens the doors of more than **200 buildings** for 48 hours: modernista townhouses, artists' studios, company headquarters, civil-war air-raid shelters. Most visits are **free**; a few need to be booked in advance, with slots released on the official site a few days before. The best free culture plan of the autumn.
