The city's main festival, held for the Virgin of la Mercè: more than 500 **free** activities across all ten districts. Expect the classics — **correfocs** (fire-runs with devils and fireworks), **castells** (human towers), parades of giants, street arts with MAC — plus two free music festivals, **BAM** and **Música Mercè**. This year's guest city is Shanghai. It all ends on Sunday with the **piromusical** fireworks show on the 27th at 10pm, launched this year from **Bogatell beach** instead of Montjuïc. The main **correfoc** is on Saturday the 26th.
