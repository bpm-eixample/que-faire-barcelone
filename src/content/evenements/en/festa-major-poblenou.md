Ten days of celebrations in one of the city's most pleasant neighbourhoods: a **castells** day, a parade of **giants**, a **correfoc** and **fireworks** launched from Bogatell beach. Most of it is **free** — a great way to experience a Catalan festa before la Mercè, with far smaller crowds.
